1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose

This document tells the developer exactly what Cityscape Books must do. It covers who uses it, what each screen does, the business rules and validations, and how each feature will be accepted. Every screenshot comes from the working prototype, which should be treated as the reference for layout, wording and behaviour.

1.2 Scope

One web application (desktop browser, plus a mobile layout for site engineers) covering:

  • Sales: quotations with revisions, proforma invoices, GST tax invoices, credit notes, client receipts & advances
  • Purchases: purchase orders, purchase bills, vendor payments, site expenses with photo proof and owner approval
  • GST: GSTR-1, GSTR-3B summary, ITC register, HSN summary, advances (Table 11A/11B), GSTR-1 JSON export
  • Cash, bank & UPI registers with statement matching
  • Client and vendor outstanding with ageing
  • Project-wise income, expenses and profit & loss, plus trial balance
  • Payroll: monthly staff, daily-wage labour, contractors
  • Inventory: material purchases by project
  • Customer support: tickets with SLA, site visits with before/after photos, warranty register, knowledge base, ratings
  • Admin panel: users, rights per module, project scoping, lock date, audit trail

1.3 How to use this document

  • This document defines behaviour, rules and acceptance criteria. Requirement IDs (FR-x.y) are for tracking in tickets and test cases.
  • The prototype defines look, copy and interaction. Open it next to this document. Its demo login lets you switch roles.
  • Technical Spec.md defines the database schema, REST API, posting rules and security controls. Where this document and the spec disagree, this document wins; raise the conflict.
  • All data in the prototype is sample data. Replace it with the opening balances and masters from Cityscape Decors (Part 9).

1.4 Definitions

GSTIN15-character GST registration number. First two digits are the state code (Tamil Nadu = 33).
CGST / SGST / IGSTSame-state supply: CGST 9% + SGST 9%. Inter-state supply: IGST 18%.
SAC 9954Service code for construction / works contract. Default on every sales line.
ITCInput tax credit, the GST on vendor bills that can be set off against output tax.
Proforma invoiceA payment request (advance or stage). Not a tax invoice and not posted to revenue.
Lock dateBooks are closed up to this date. Nothing dated on or before it can be created, edited or deleted.
SLAService level agreement: the time allowed to first respond to and to resolve a support ticket.
AMCAnnual maintenance contract with a client after handover.
Pending / ApprovedExpenses entered by non-approvers are Pending until the owner approves. Pending entries are excluded from all balances and reports.

3.2 GST

IDRequirement
FR-3.2.1Company state code is 33 (Tamil Nadu). If the client’s state code is 33, the invoice charges CGST 9% + SGST 9%; otherwise IGST 18%. The same rule applies to quotations and proformas.
FR-3.2.2For vendor bills and POs, the split is decided by the vendor GSTIN’s first two digits. A vendor with no GSTIN carries no GST and gives no input credit.
FR-3.2.3Default SAC is 9954 on every sales line; it can be overridden per line.
FR-3.2.4GSTIN format is validated with ^\d{2}[A-Z]{5}\d{4}[A-Z][1-9A-Z]Z[0-9A-Z]$ and the first two digits must match the chosen state.
FR-3.2.5Amounts in words use the Indian system (Crore, Lakh, Thousand).

3.3 Lock date, approvals and audit

IDRequirement
FR-3.3.1The owner sets a books lock date (Users & security → Security). Any create, edit or delete dated on or before it is refused with “Books are locked till DD MMM YYYY. Pick a later date.”
FR-3.3.2Expenses saved by anyone without the Approve right are Pending. Pending entries are excluded from project balances, banking, reports, P&L and GST until approved. Editing an approved expense without the Approve right sends it back to Pending.
FR-3.3.3Every create, edit, delete, approval, rejection, payment, sign-in, rights change and lock change writes an append-only audit row (when, who, what, before/after).
FR-3.3.4Tax invoices are never deleted. They are cancelled with a credit note. Tickets are never deleted; they are closed.

3.4 Shell: navigation, search, notifications

IDRequirement
FR-3.4.1Left navigation is grouped: Dashboard, Projects, Clients & vendors · Sales · Purchases · Accounts · Support · Operations. Items the user has no right to are hidden.
FR-3.4.2Global search (2+ characters) finds projects, quotes, proformas, POs, invoices, bills, expenses and tickets the user can see.
FR-3.4.3+ New menu: Expense, Client receipt, Quotation, Proforma, Tax invoice, Purchase order, Purchase bill, Support ticket, Project, filtered by the Edit right.
FR-3.4.4In-app notifications (bell with unread count): expenses awaiting approval, project budget ≥85% or over 100%, quote expiring in ≤3 days or expired, overdue proforma, overdue invoice, bill due within 7 days, ticket SLA breached or due soon.
FR-3.4.5Every list and report has Export CSV. Invoices, quotations, proformas, POs and payslips have Print / PDF in A4 layout.
FR-3.4.6Formatting: ₹ with lakh/crore grouping (en-IN), dates as DD MMM YYYY, FY April–March.

4. Module requirements

Each module lists its purpose, the screen, numbered functional requirements and acceptance criteria. Requirement IDs are FR-<module>.<n>.

4.1 Login

IDRequirement
FR-1.1Single-step login with work email and password. No SMS or OTP.
FR-1.25 failed attempts lock the account for 15 minutes and notify the owner. New users set their password from an emailed invite link; “Forgot password” sends a reset link by email (single-use, 24-hour expiry).
FR-1.3Sessions end after 30 minutes idle. Deactivated users cannot sign in, but their entries remain in the books and audit trail.
FR-1.4Site Engineers land on the mobile layout; all other roles land on the desktop dashboard.
Acceptance criteria
  • A wrong password shows an error and does not sign in; the 5th failure locks the account for 15 minutes.
  • A deactivated user is refused at step 1.

FR-2.2KPI cards: Client receivables, Vendor payables, Cash & bank balance (banking right), then Waiting for your approval (approvers) or Collected this year.
FR-2.3Projects table: project, invoice value, Income · Cr, Expense · Dr, Net value only with the profit right, budget-used bar. Clicking a row opens the project.

4.3 Projects & project ledger

IDRequirement
FR-3.1List filter chips All / Active / Completed; columns ID, Project · client, Site, Status, Contract, Received, Budget used. “+ New project” needs Projects ≥ Edit.
FR-3.2New project: name, client (pick or add), site, contract value, cost budget (required, drives overspend alerts), engineer, manager.
FR-3.3Project header: code, status, name, client · site · start · engineer · manager. Actions: Record receipt, Edit project, New proforma, New invoice, + Add expense (each by right).
FR-3.4Stats strip: Contract value, Invoiced (incl. GST), Received · Cr, Spent · Dr, Balance in hand, Profit so far (profit right only), Budget used.
FR-3.5Ledger tab reproduces the client’s Excel sheet: S.No, Date, Particulars, Type, Receipts, Payments, Paid by, Mode, Paid to, Bill/ref, running Balance. Running balance counts approved entries only; pending rows are tinted and labelled “Awaiting owner approval”.

4.5 Quotations

IDRequirement
FR-5.1List chips All / Draft / Sent / Accepted / Declined / Expired; cards Open quotes, Accepted, Win rate (accepted ÷ accepted + declined + expired).
FR-5.2Form: client, proposal title, site, quote date, valid till, discount % (0–50), line grid (description, qty, unit, rate), terms. Live totals with the GST split from the client’s state.
FR-5.3Status flow Draft → Sent → Accepted / Declined. A Sent quote past “valid till” shows as Expired (derived, not stored).
FR-5.4Revise keeps the number, increments the revision, stores the previous version (rev, date, total, status) in history and resets to Draft. The view shows full revision history.
FR-5.5Accept opens “Accept quotation & create project”: name and site pre-filled, contract value = quoted taxable value, cost budget (default 75%) with live planned margin. Creates the project linked to the quote.
FR-5.6From an accepted quote: Create proforma (30/40/50/100% of the quote) or Create tax invoice (lines copied, discount applied to rates).
FR-5.7A service quotation raised from a support ticket links to the ticket’s existing project when accepted and does not create a new project.
FR-5.8Only Draft quotes can be deleted (Quotations = Full).

4.6 Proforma invoices

IDRequirement
FR-6.1A proforma is a payment request for an advance or stage. It is not a tax invoice and is excluded from receivables, ageing, revenue, P&L and the GSTR-1 B2B table.
FR-6.2List chips All / Draft / Sent / Partially paid / Paid / Overdue / Converted / Cancelled; cards Awaiting payment, Advances held, Converted.
FR-6.3Form: project, stage name (e.g. Advance 30%), date, payment due, lines. GST split from the project’s client.
FR-6.4“Record advance” records a receipt against the proforma. It is held as a customer advance (liability) and shown on the project as “₹X held as customer advance”.
FR-6.5Convert to tax invoice creates the next CSD/26-27/#### dated today with the same lines, moves every advance onto the new invoice, and marks the proforma Converted.
FR-6.6Edit and Cancel are allowed only while no advance has been received.
FR-6.7GST on advances: tax is due when an advance is received. It is reported in GSTR-1 Table 11A and reversed in 11B in the month the invoice is raised (see 4.15).
Acceptance criteria
  • After conversion, the invoice shows the advance as already received and its balance due is total minus advance.
  • A proforma never appears in Outstanding.

FR-7.3Edit only when there are no receipts and the date is after the lock date.
FR-7.4Cancel (Invoices = Full, no receipts) issues credit note CN/26-27/####; the invoice stays on record as Cancelled.

4.15 GST returns

IDRequirement

4.16 Reports & trial balance

IDRequirement

4.19 Support tickets

An internal service desk modelled on Zoho Desk. Clients do not log in; staff log every call, walk-in or message. Handlers are the Owner and the Asst. Manager.

IDRequirement
FR-19.1Ticket types: Snag / defect, Warranty claim, Service & repair, AMC visit, Billing query, Change request. Received via: Phone call, Walk-in, WhatsApp, Email, Site visit.
FR-19.2Log ticket form: project, type, coverage (shown live), subject, description, priority, received via, caller name and phone, assign to.
FR-19.3Statuses: Open → In progress → Awaiting client / Visit scheduled → Resolved → Closed. Reopening clears the resolved time and rating. A ticket cannot be resolved while a visit is still scheduled.
FR-19.4SLA by priority (calendar hours from logging). First response: Urgent 1h, High 4h, Normal 8h, Low 24h. Resolution: Urgent 4h, High 24h, Normal 3 days, Low 7 days.
FR-19.5The first logged reply or call stamps the first response time; internal notes do not. SLA shows Due in…, Overdue by…, Responded in… or Missed.
FR-19.6Queue: cards Open, SLA breached, Visits scheduled, Awaiting client; views Open, Assigned to me, SLA breached, Unassigned, Resolved, Closed, All; breached tickets sort first.
FR-19.7Coverage is decided when the ticket is logged: within the project warranty = In warranty (free); after warranty = chargeable; not yet handed over = fix under contract; AMC visit = covered while AMC is active; change request = chargeable.
IDRequirement
FR-19.8Activity thread with system events, Reply to client, Call log and Internal note. Composer can insert a reply template (placeholders {client}, {ticket}, {project}, {engineer}, {date} filled from the ticket) or a care guide.
FR-19.9Schedule site visit: date, slot (Morning 9–12, Afternoon 12–4, Evening 4–7), site engineer, instructions. Ticket becomes Visit scheduled. Visits can be cancelled.
FR-19.10Billing card: chargeable / free toggle; Create quotation and Raise tax invoice (both linked to the ticket); list of linked documents and costs charged to the project.
FR-19.11Customer rating 1–5 stars with comment, recorded by staff after Resolved/Closed.
FR-19.12Support dashboard: average first response, average resolution, response and resolution SLA met %, customer rating, service cost charged to projects; charts by type, status and project; team workload; SLA targets.
Acceptance criteria
  • A High ticket logged at 11:00 with no reply shows “No response · overdue” after 15:00 and raises a notification.
  • Completing a visit with a ₹1,200 cost creates a Pending expense on the ticket’s project that appears in Approvals.
  • A ticket on a project whose warranty ended is marked chargeable at creation.
